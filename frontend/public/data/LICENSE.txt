Coordinates identify actual mapped airport features. Presentation routes,
simulation stands and aircraft motion do not constitute operational routing,
airport clearance, live telemetry or a certified aerodrome chart.

The historical flight/replay fixtures are separate from the OpenStreetMap data;
their supplied source provenance is retained in the project dataset contract.
